How it works

Open a model — everything else is at your fingertips.

  1. Rotate and zoom

    Drag to rotate, pinch or scroll to zoom. A more detailed level loads automatically as you get closer.

  2. Camera presets

    Jump between perspective, façade, bird’s-eye and plan views with one tap, or start the cinematic tour.

  3. Measure and capture

    Measure the distance between two points, save a signed screenshot and share a link to exactly what you are looking at.

  4. See it in your space

    On supported Android and iOS devices, place the model on your desk or floor with augmented reality.

  5. Use it offline

    Save a building to your device and it opens without a connection. The site can also be installed as an app.

  6. Find it on the map

    Pick a building on the campus map, see where it is and get directions with one tap.
